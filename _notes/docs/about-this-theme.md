A digital garden is a website that grows instead of being published once. Notes begin as **seedlings**, get tended into **budding** notes, and settle as **evergreen** pages you keep maintaining.

This template gives you that structure on Jekyll with no JavaScript framework and no build step beyond `jekyll build`.

## Design in one paragraph
Monospace type, white ground, near-black ink, hairline borders, zero radius, no shadows. One accent, signal green, is reserved for state: the featured panel, "evergreen", live and reveal. If everything is green, nothing is.

## What you get
- Six collections: notes, essays, log, projects, reading, glossary
- Nested folders that become URLs and breadcrumbs
- Two-level taxonomy (topics and tags) plus growth stages
- Backlinks computed at build time, with no plugin
- Embed and callout includes
- Client-side search and an auto-built table of contents
