{% raw %}
1. **Capture** a seedling in under a minute. A title and three sentences are enough.
2. **Link** it to at least one existing note.
3. **Tend** weekly: pick the oldest seedlings, improve one, bump `updated`.
4. **Promote** a note to *evergreen* only when you'd be happy to send it to someone.
5. **Prune** anything you no longer believe, or mark it with a callout.

Use `draft: true` for anything you aren't ready to show.
