Five axes, from coarse to fine:

1. **Collection** is the kind of thing: note, essay, log entry, project, book, term.
2. **Folder** is optional grouping inside a collection.
3. **Topic** is a broad shelf. Keep the list short and stable.
4. **Tag** is a specific handle. Add freely; prune quarterly.
5. **Stage** is maturity: seedling, budding, evergreen.

## Pages generated for you
- `/tags/` lists every topic and tag with its documents.
- `/stages/` groups everything by maturity.
- `/tags/<tag>/` and `/topics/<topic>/` pages are generated by `_plugins/taxonomy.rb`. They need a builder that runs plugins (not the default GitHub Pages one).

## Adding a new axis
Add a field (say `people: [ada]`), then copy the loop in `tags.html`, replacing `topics` with the new key. See [Scaling your garden]({{ "/notes/docs/scaling-your-garden/" | relative_url }}).
